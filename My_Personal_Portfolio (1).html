It gives evidence-informed self-care information, helps users work out what level of professional support they need, and connects them quickly to qualified people when a situation looks concerning. It does not diagnose, prescribe or replace healthcare professionals.

LearnGap

I built LearnGap, an education AI solution for Grade 8 maths, as a working prototype with a written brief. A learner takes a ten-question adaptive check, and each answer decides the next question. It then drafts a short study plan with a plain-language reason for every item

Open LearnGap

Inventory Analysis

Inventory Analysis is a browser tool that helps businesses avoid overstocking and understocking. Upload a CSV of your items and sales, and it forecasts demand, flags each item as understocked, overstocked or healthy, and estimates the profit at risk and the cash tied up in excess stock. It scores customer reviews with sentiment analysis, shows the results in charts, tables and pop-ups, and drafts a report you can add your own interpretation to.

Open Inventory Analysis
